PART 4 — PROBATION AND CONFIRMATION

4.0 Scope. Every employee appointed to a permanent position is appointed on
probation, unless the appointment letter expressly says otherwise.

4.1 Duration of probation. The period of probation is 180 days from the date of
joining. Days of unpaid leave availed during probation do not count towards the
180 days, and the probation is treated as extended by an equal number of days
without any further communication.

4.2 Confirmation review. The reporting manager shall complete a written
confirmation review not later than 15 days before the probation ends. The review
records whether the employee has met the objectives set at the time of joining
and recommends confirmation, extension or separation.

4.3 Extension of probation. Probation may be extended in the circumstances and
on the terms stated in the regional annexure applicable to the employee's
office. This Part does not itself state the length of an extension, the number
of times probation may be extended, or any allowance payable during an
extension.

4.4 Confirmation. On confirmation, the Company issues a written confirmation
letter. The employee is a confirmed employee from the date stated in that
letter, and Part 3 clause 3.1 applies from that date.

4.5 Benefits during probation. An employee on probation accrues earned leave
under clause 2.1 but may not avail earned leave during probation without the
written approval of the department head. Casual leave and sick leave are
available during probation on the same terms as for a confirmed employee.

4.6 No automatic confirmation. Probation does not end automatically. Employment
continues on probation terms until a written confirmation letter is issued.
