PART 2 — LEAVE POLICY

2.0 Scope. This Part applies to every confirmed employee of Vaanam Technologies
Private Limited ("the Company") on the rolls of any Indian office. Contractors,
interns and consultants engaged through a third-party vendor are outside the
scope of this Part and are governed by their engagement letter.

2.1 Annual earned leave. A confirmed employee accrues 18 days of earned leave
per completed leave year. The leave year runs from 1 April to 31 March. Earned
leave accrues at the rate of 1.5 days for each completed calendar month of
service and is credited to the employee's leave account on the first day of the
following month.

2.2 Application and approval. An employee shall apply for earned leave through
the leave-management system. An application for three or more consecutive
working days requires the written approval of the reporting manager and of the
department head. The Company may decline a leave application where the absence
would materially affect a customer commitment, and shall record the reason in
writing.

2.3 Leave during notice. Earned leave shall not be applied for or availed during
the notice period referred to in Part 3, except with the written approval of the
department head. Leave availed during the notice period does not extend the
notice period unless the approval expressly says so.

2.4 Carry-forward. An employee may carry forward a maximum of 10 days of unused
earned leave into the next leave year. Any balance above 10 days as on 31 March
lapses and is not payable.

2.5 Encashment. On separation, the unused earned leave standing to the credit of
the employee, up to a maximum of 30 days, is encashed at the employee's basic
salary. Allowances, variable pay and reimbursements are excluded from the
encashment computation.

2.6 Casual leave. An employee is entitled to 7 days of casual leave per leave
year. Casual leave may not be combined with earned leave, does not carry forward
and is not encashed.

2.7 Sick leave. An employee is entitled to 12 days of sick leave per leave year.
A medical certificate from a registered medical practitioner is required for an
absence of more than 2 consecutive working days on sick leave.

2.8 Public holidays. The Company publishes a list of 10 public holidays for each
calendar year. The list is published by the Human Resources department not later
than 15 December of the preceding year.

2.9 Regional annexures. The Company may issue a regional leave annexure for a
particular office. Where a regional annexure is in force, the annexure prevails
over this Part to the extent of any inconsistency, for employees on the rolls of
that office. A regional annexure is issued in the principal language of the
office to which it applies and is binding in that language.

2.10 Unpaid leave. Leave without pay may be sanctioned only by the department
head and only after the employee's earned leave and casual leave balances are
exhausted.
