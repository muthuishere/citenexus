PART 9 — CONFIDENTIALITY AND INTELLECTUAL PROPERTY

9.0 Confidential information. "Confidential information" means any non-public
information of the Company or of a customer of the Company that the employee
receives, creates or has access to in the course of employment, including source
code, architecture documents, customer lists, pricing, unreleased product plans,
security findings and personnel data.

9.2 Obligation. An employee shall not disclose confidential information to any
person outside the Company, and shall not use it for any purpose other than
performing the employee's duties. The obligation applies during employment and
continues after the employment ends. The period for which the obligation
continues after employment, and the consequences of a breach, are stated in the
confidentiality annexure applicable to the employee's office; this Part does not
itself state that period or those consequences.

9.3 Company property. All notes, drawings, models, records and copies of
confidential information, in any medium, are the property of the Company and
shall be returned or destroyed on the last working day.

9.5 Intellectual property. Every invention, design, work of authorship and
improvement made by an employee in the course of employment, or using Company
resources, vests in the Company. The employee shall sign any document reasonably
required to record that vesting.

9.6 Permitted disclosure. Nothing in this Part prevents an employee from making
a disclosure required by law, or a good-faith report to a regulator or to the
Company's whistle-blowing channel. An employee shall inform the Company's legal
counsel of a disclosure required by law before making it, unless prevented from
doing so by law.

9.7 Personal devices. An employee shall not store confidential information on a
personal device or a personal cloud account. Where remote access is approved,
access is only through the Company's managed virtual desktop.

9.8 Third-party confidential information. An employee shall not bring into the
Company, or use in the course of employment, confidential information belonging
to a previous employer or to any third party.
