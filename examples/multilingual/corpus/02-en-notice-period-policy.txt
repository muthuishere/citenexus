PART 3 — SEPARATION AND NOTICE PERIOD

3.0 Scope. This Part governs resignation by an employee and termination by the
Company, other than termination for misconduct, which is governed by Part 8.

3.1 Notice by a confirmed employee. A confirmed employee who wishes to resign
shall give the Company 60 days written notice. The notice period begins on the
working day following the day on which the resignation is acknowledged in the
human-resources system, and not on the day the employee informs the reporting
manager verbally.

3.2 Notice during probation. An employee who is on probation shall give 30 days
written notice. The Company may likewise end the employment of an employee on
probation on 30 days written notice, or on payment of salary in lieu of that
notice.

3.3 Notice by the Company. Where the Company ends the employment of a confirmed
employee for reasons other than misconduct, the Company shall give 60 days
written notice or salary in lieu of that notice, computed on the fixed monthly
gross salary.

3.4 Notice-period buy-out. The Company is not obliged to accept a shortened
notice period. Where a regional annexure provides a buy-out mechanism, the
mechanism in that annexure applies to employees on the rolls of the office to
which the annexure is issued. This Part does not itself state a buy-out amount.

3.5 Regional annexures prevail. Where a regional annexure issued under clause
2.9 or under this Part is in force for an office, the annexure prevails over
this Part to the extent of any inconsistency, for employees on the rolls of that
office. The annexure is binding in the language in which it is issued.

3.6 Handover. During the notice period the employee shall complete a written
handover of all work in progress, credentials, customer contacts and Company
property to a person nominated by the department head. Full and final settlement
is released not later than 45 days after the last working day, and is withheld
where the handover is incomplete.

3.7 Retention of records. The Company retains separation records for 7 years
from the last working day.

3.8 Re-employment. A former employee who resigned with notice duly served is
eligible to apply for re-employment after 6 months. An employee whose employment
was ended for misconduct is not eligible for re-employment.
